# Known Issues & Tribal Knowledge

This document captures real-world user experiences, common problems, and operational insights gathered from forums, reviews, and field reports.

---

## 1. Critical Design Issues

### 1.1 Water Not Linked to Motor ⚠️ DESIGN FLAW

**Problem**: When the auger jams, the motor stops but water continues spraying.

**Impact**:
- Results in extremely inconsistent mix (too wet in some areas)
- Wastes water
- Requires manual valve shutoff during jams

**User Quote**: "There is a significant design flaw: the water is not linked to the motor. So, when you jam up the water will continue to spray which results in extremely inconsistent mixing."

**Workaround**:
- Keep hand near water dial when operating
- Immediately shut off water when toggling forward/reverse

---

### 1.2 Hopper Clogging / Material Bridging ⚠️ COMMON

**Problem**: Dry concrete mix gets stuck in the hopper, causing inconsistent flow to the auger.

**Causes**:
- Hopper angle design
- Damp or chunky concrete mix
- High humidity conditions

**User Quote**: "The dry material will get stuck in the hopper and not feed to the auger. You have to constantly shake the hopper as you feed it more dry mix."

**Impact**:
- One user lost 20 minutes shoveling out compacted hopper
- Concrete in forms started hardening before pour complete

**Workarounds**:
- Use fresh, dry bagged mix (Quikrete recommended)
- Constantly agitate hopper during operation
- Consider hopper extension for better material flow
- Some users suggest MudMixer needs a secondary auger/agitator in hopper

---

### 1.3 Large Aggregate Jamming ⚠️ STRICT LIMIT

**Problem**: Aggregate larger than 1/2 inch causes auger jamming.

**Impact**:
- "Constant toggling between forward and reverse to clear jams"
- "Ruins your day" if aggregate is too large
- Machine includes shear pin to protect auger

**User Quote**: "If you have any large aggregate in the mix it will ruin your day. You constantly have to toggle between forward and reverse to clear the jam."

**Prevention**:
- ONLY use mixes with aggregate ≤1/2 inch (12.7mm)
- Avoid pea gravel mixes unless verified
- Check bag specifications before use

## 2. Water Control Issues

### 2.1 Inconsistent Water Dial

**Problem**: Water dial can be "finicky" with inconsistent flow.

**User Quote**: "The water valve is a little finicky. Sometimes it goes from wet to dry with no change to the dial."

**Contributing Factors**:
- Pressure variations in water supply
- Dial calibration
- Nozzle clogging

**Workarounds**:
- Ensure consistent 40+ PSI water pressure
- Clean nozzles regularly
- Dial in water setting before starting pour

### 2.2 Constant Wet/Dry Adjustment

**Problem**: Requires constant monitoring and adjustment.

**User Quote**: "The constant too wet/too dry is a negative you need to constantly deal with."

**Reality Check**: Not a "set it and forget it" machine.

## 3. Electrical Issues

### 3.1 GFCI/Power Problems

**Problem**: Ground fault protector issues on some units.

**User Reports**:
- "Had a problem with the ground fault protector - machine would not turn on without tapping on it"
- Water control not activating on third use
- Requires physical tapping to reset

**Note**: Evolution model (MMXR-3225) has upgraded, certified GFCI.

### 3.2 Parts Durability

**User Report**: "This mixer has been used 5 times and have almost replaced every part on mixer. Company has sent me parts for free but have to install them on my time and my dollar."

**Common Wear Items** (per Spare Parts Kit):
- 90-degree elbows
- Auger switch
- GFCI cord
- Auger (complete replacement available)
- Auger pins

## 4. Operational Limitations

### 4.1 Cannot Stop Mid-Pour

**Problem**: Must be continuously fed and dumped.

**User Quote**: "The MudMixer needs to be constantly fed and dumped and not stopped, or if you do stop, you have to clean it all out so it doesn't set in the machine."

**Impact**:
- Not suitable for stop-and-go work (e.g., setting pavers)
- Requires immediate cleanup if stopped

### 4.2 Two-Person Crew Recommended

**Problem**: Solo operation can be challenging.

**User Quote**: "When using the mixer, it's best to have two people to help keep things moving. Doing this solo may hinder you from completing everything you need to."

**Optimal Workflow**:
- Person 1: Feed bags, manage hopper
- Person 2: Direct output, work forms

### 4.3 Speed Complaints

**User Quote**: "The mixer is so slow it retards production. If the mixer was 3-4 times faster, the mudmixer would be an amazing product."

**Note**: MudMixer Pro (2026) addresses this with 3× throughput.

## 5. Material Compatibility Issues

### 5.1 Materials That DON'T Work

| Material | Status | Reason |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Self-leveling products | ❌ NO | Requires high-shear mixing |
| Air crete / Foam crete | ❌ NO | Foam incompatible with auger |
| Epoxy products | ❌ NO | Different mixing requirements |
| Straight Portland cement | ❌ NO | Must have sand/gravel |
| Hemp products | ❌ NO | Fiber may jam auger |

### 5.2 Damp/Chunky Mix Problems

**Problem**: Slightly damp or chunky concrete causes major issues.

**User Quote**: "If your concrete has gotten slightly damp or is a little chunky then you will have problems and it will not mix consistently. The chunks get in the way of the auger and ruins the feed, making it watery."

**Prevention**:
- Store bags in dry location
- Check bag condition before use
- Break up chunks manually before loading

### 5.3 Mortar Feed Issues

**Problem**: Mortar is too fine to feed well.

**User Quote**: "Mortar is too fine to feed well - creates air pockets and comes out watery."

### 5.4 Colored Concrete Issues

**Problem**: Pigment doesn't distribute evenly.

**User Quote**: "The mixer does not hold and mix long enough to properly distribute the pigment throughout the mix."

**Workaround**: Pre-mix pigment into dry material before loading.

## 7. When NOT to Use MudMixer

Based on user feedback, MudMixer may not be ideal for:

| Scenario | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Jobs > 2 yards | Ready-mix truck more economical |
| House flipping (varied work) | "Carting this around isn't ideal" |
| Intermittent pours (pavers) | Cannot pause operation |
| Very small batches | Overkill, requires cleanup |
| Off-grid (no power) | Requires 120V AC |
| Low water pressure sites | Needs 30+ PSI |

---

## 8. When MudMixer Excels

| Scenario | Why |

| Remote locations | Portable, fits in SUV |
